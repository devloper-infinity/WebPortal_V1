# Helpdesk process

Roles: Requester, IT Agent, IT Supervisor, Helpdesk Admin, and configured Approver.

`New → Pending Approval (when configured) → Assigned → In Progress → Waiting for User/Vendor → Resolved → requester-confirmed Closed`

Rejected, Cancelled, and Reopened are controlled exception paths. Category approval
rules, impact/urgency priority, SLA response/resolution targets, department queues,
assignment, internal notes, requester replies, and every transition are enforced
server-side and recorded in the audit timeline.
